Would?

2026/11/14

Last summer I was doing an internship in Seattle. It started like any regular day: I woke up, got ready, and randomly decided to wear my Alice in Chains shirt. When I opened the door to head upstairs, John saw me and said, “Alice in Chains!!”

He was psyched to see I’m a fan, and he told me how much he listened to them when he was younger. That made me even more psyched. He played acoustic guitar and I had my bass with me, so we practiced “Would?” a few times. We never fully nailed it, but it’s still one of my best memories from that summer. When I was leaving Seattle (it was raining heavily that morning, unsurprisingly), he said he wished we could’ve practiced more Alice in Chains songs.

I have a few of their CDs in my car. Whenever I listen to one of their songs, especially “Whale & Wasp,” I get thrown right back to that summer: driving around Ravenna in my Mustang, knowing Layne Staley used to be around here. His apartment was in the U District, not far from where I live.

I’m still practicing the bassline to “Would?”. It’s far from perfect, but it’s getting there.

Oh, by the way, John had a really nice voice. I remember him singing at the Tuesday music nights. The more I listen to Layne Staley and Kurt Cobain (the chorus of “Down in a Hole,” the chorus of “Would?,” “Smells Like Teen Spirit”), the more I realize you gotta be a white person from the PNW to sing like that. It’s hard to describe what I mean, but I truly feel it. John was from the PNW too.
