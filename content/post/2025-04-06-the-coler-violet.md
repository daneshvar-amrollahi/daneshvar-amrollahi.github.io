Recently, I've become quite obsessed with the song [The Color Violet - Tory Lanez](https://open.spotify.com/track/3azJifCSqg9fRij2yKIbWz?si=82b7c5cbdcee452d). Although at first glance it may come across as more of a teenagery(?) song—which I'm generally not interested in—I truly enjoy the feeling of listening to it and admire the way it conveys particular concepts through its lyrics.

The song explores the tension between desire and self-protection, portraying a character caught between chasing connection and keeping their guard up. Beneath a surface of fast living and emotional distance lies a quieter longing for something real. I won’t elaborate further on the lyrics; I’ll leave that as an exercise to the listener. Honestly, I believe listening to it at night when it's mostly dark and all you see are some tiny lights and there's motion happening is the best setup for it. It's quite an emotionally intense experience that I rarely find in other songs.
